How to Play

Create or Join

Enter your name, choose a game, and create a battle room. Share the room code with your friends.

Lobby

Players join the same room and can use the lobby chat. The host starts the battle when everyone is ready.

Battle

Game logic is server-authoritative. Invalid moves are rejected and scores stay synchronized across players.

Rematch

After the result, the host can send everyone back to the lobby for another match.
